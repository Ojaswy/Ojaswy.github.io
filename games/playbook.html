01

The formula

Strip Meccha and the whole "friendslop" gold rush down to parts. Every concept below is built from these eight. Miss one and the clip doesn't write itself.

01

One-sentence twist

If you can't explain it in a breath, it's not a free tutorial. "Hide & seek, but you paint yourself."

02

Fix the original's flaw

Hiding is boring → painting is funny. Find what made the kids' game drag and delete it.

03

Betrayal & blame

The game must let friends screw each other. Shared blame is what people scream about on camera.

04

Readable, funny failure

The funniest moment must also be the most shareable screenshot. Bad outcome = instant comedy.

05

Chat = judge + saboteur

Twitch chat votes and meddles. Channel-point participation is what locked in the streamers.

06

The auto-clip engine

Build one repeatable moment the game spits out as a shareable artifact. That's free marketing.

07

Below the friction price

$3.99–$4.99. The viewer buys it mid-stream without thinking. Friction kills the impulse.

08

Blobs > fidelity

Stick figures and blobs. Meccha proved the mechanic carries it. Spend zero on polish.

02

The hero game

Curse Tag + Public Trial + Haunted + Elementary, merged onto the one childhood game with a perfect built-in clip rhythm: musical chairs.

★ HERO PICK · highest ceiling

HOT SEAT

Musical chairs — the trial. The chair you fight for and the seat you're judged in are the same seat.

One sentence: Musical chairs, but losing doesn't eliminate you — it curses you, and the whole lobby (plus chat) votes how badly.

The fix that makes it work

Real musical chairs has a fatal flaw: losers are eliminated and sit there bored. Hot Seat deletes that flaw. The loser isn't out — they're cursed and keep playing, falling apart a little more each round. Nobody leaves. Losing just gets funnier the more it happens. (Same move Meccha made on "hiding is boring.")

The 90-second loop

01
Scramble

N players, N-1 physics chairs. Music plays, everyone circles.

02
Dive

Music stops (chat can trigger it). Everyone ragdoll-dives for a seat.

03
Hot seat

The chairless loser is strapped in. Lobby + chat vote: mercy or curse.

04
Curse

Curse drawn — tilt screen, invert, drunk-legs. It stacks and lingers.

05
Reveal

You see exactly who voted to curse you. Revenge starts now.

How the four ideas merge in

CURSE TAG
↓

Curses stack & linger. By the final round the worst player is gloriously unplayable.

PUBLIC TRIAL
↓

The hot-seat vote strips a mechanic. Chat is the judge; conviction = a debuff.

HAUNTED
↓

The reveal names who cursed you. Vendetta spiral — shove them next round.

ELEMENTARY
↓

Most-cursed player becomes "The Gremlin" — a theatrical villain + an auto-roast clip.

Why it rages

  • Twist: explained in one breath — free tutorial.
  • Betrayal: you vote to curse friends, then shove them off chairs.
  • Failure: the Gremlin face-planting an empty chair is the screenshot.
  • Participation: chat is the judge and the chair thief.

03

The portfolio

Eight childhood games → clippable modern versions, ranked by viral ceiling. Hot Seat is the bet; these are the bench (and your sequel pipeline).

THRONE
King of the Hill
The longer you hold the throne, the heavier and clumsier you get — while everyone else gets a "regicide" speed buff.
Clip: the smug leader dogpiled off the hill in slow motion.
Betrayal: alliances form to dethrone #1, then instantly collapse.
Chat: grease the hill, or drop a buff for the underdog.
Viral ceiling · very high
FUSE
Hot Potato
Pass a live bomb. You can fake the throw. Chat can see the timer — you can't.
Clip: the ragdoll launch when it blows in your hands.
Betrayal: force a catch on someone with one second left.
Chat: secretly speed up the fuse for a chosen victim.
Viral ceiling · high
POLO
Marco Polo
Everyone's blindfolded (black screen + 3D audio) except one traitor who can see — and lies about being blind.
Clip: walking confidently off a ledge into the void.
Betrayal: the seer imitates other players' voices.
Chat: shout fake "Polo!" pings to misdirect.
Viral ceiling · high
CURSE TAG
Tag / Freeze Tag
Being "it" visibly corrupts your controls, and a residue lingers after you pass it on — everyone ends slightly broken.
Clip: the tilted-screen meltdown of someone "it" too long.
Betrayal: the lobby ganging up to keep one person "it".
Chat: picks who starts the match cursed.
Viral ceiling · high · (DNA folded into Hot Seat)
SARDINES
Sardines (reverse hide & seek)
One person hides; everyone who finds them must silently cram into the same spot. Proximity voice rises — giggles betray you.
Clip: eight blobs stuffed in a locker, one idiot blowing it.
Betrayal: loudly "accidentally" revealing the spot.
Chat: ping a noise that force-triggers your mic.
Viral ceiling · medium-high · closest Meccha cousin
SIMON
Simon Says
Simon is a player who wins by baiting you into moving — and everyone who's out becomes a ghost-Simon who can also bark fake commands.
Clip: the whole lobby twitching at contradictory orders.
Betrayal: ghost-Simons hunting the one who got them out.
Chat: bribe Simon to target a specific player.
Viral ceiling · medium-high
ROPE
Tug of War
You can cut the rope, or defect to the other side mid-pull. The losing team ragdoll-faceplants into the mud.
Clip: the synchronized faceplant; the traitor's smug walk-over.
Betrayal: literally switching sides at the last second.
Chat: votes one secret saboteur onto each team.
Viral ceiling · medium

04

Bonus bet — public-domain IP

A different genre (social deduction) and a second, lower-risk shot on goal — because Sherlock Holmes is free and the role asymmetry is free tutorial.

ELEMENTARY
One Moriarty. Everyone else is Holmes.
deduction · $3.99

Moriarty sabotages the objective; the Holmeses must deduce who. The twist: you can't just vote — you spend a deduction token to accuse with a stated reason. Wrong accusation → Moriarty picks your debuff (screen flips, sepia, half-speed). The killer feature: after each round an animated Holmes delivers an auto-generated post-mortem roast of the worst player ("You walked into the same pillar twice. Your accusation was baseless. You are, demonstrably, why Moriarty wins. Elementary."). Unlike Among Us's anonymous imposter, Moriarty is named, theatrical, and has a victory monologue — streamers will fight to be him. Art: stick figures in deerstalker hats; the gap between low-fi art and dead-serious dialogue is the joke.

05

Execution — non-negotiable

The game that wins isn't the most polished. It's the one where the funniest moment is also the most shareable screenshot — shipped cheap and chat-native.

Streamer lobby codes

Friends + viewers join in one click. No friction, no account walls.

Twitch channel-point voting

Chat votes and sabotages every round. This is what converts viewers to buyers.

Price $4.99 ($3.99 deduction)

Below the impulse threshold. Bought mid-stream without a second thought.

Blob / stick-figure art

The mechanic carries it. Keep dev cost near zero; every screenshot funnier for it.

The auto-clip is the product

The roast / face-plant clip is the marketing unit. Build the share button first.

Ship one arena

One map, ~8 curses, ragdoll physics. Replayability comes from people, not content.
